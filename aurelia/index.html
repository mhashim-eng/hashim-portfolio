How do I make an appointment?

Choose your service, provider and a time on the booking page. This public demo creates a local simulation, not a real studio reservation.

Where is the studio?

Miami, Florida is the fictional demo location. A genuine street address will be added after business approval.

Can I change my appointment?

The sample policy allows changes at least 24 hours before the appointment. Production bookings use the private management reference and code supplied in the confirmation email.

Which service is right for me?

Start with a consultation and discuss your questions with an appropriately qualified professional. This website does not provide personalized treatment advice.

Are prices final?

All displayed prices and services are fictional sample values. No payment is taken in the demo.
