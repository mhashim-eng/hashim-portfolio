Booking policy — demo

Appointments in the public demo are simulations. Production defaults: book at least 2 hours ahead, up to 90 days ahead; change or cancel at least 24 hours before the appointment. Pay at studio; no deposit is collected. These are proposed rules, not a final business agreement. Contact the studio for changes inside the cutoff.

Privacy notice — demo

The static demo keeps fictional appointment schedules and reference details in this browser. Contact details are not retained after a demo booking. Use Clear demo appointments to remove the schedule. Contact and newsletter previews do not transmit data. Hosting providers may maintain ordinary access logs. The WordPress version stores appointment and inquiry contact details in an administrator-restricted database only after live mode is configured. Do not enter diagnoses, medical history or other sensitive health information. A production privacy notice must identify the actual business, providers, retention and contact channels.

Cookie notice — demo

No advertising or analytics cookies are added by this demo. Browser storage is used for the simulated appointment schedule. WordPress uses essential authentication cookies for its dashboard. Additional integrations require a fresh cookie and privacy review.

Terms of use — demo

Aurelia is fictional. Sample services, prices, profiles and images are for portfolio review. This is not a medical service, professional advice, a payment facility or an operating clinic. Final business terms must be approved before launch.
